# The City - Setting Reference

## Overview
The City is a massive urban complex the size of a small country with a population of around 7 billion. Bordered to the south by the Great Lake and to all other directions by the Outskirts. The Ruins lie underneath it.

---

## Districts & Governance

### Structure
- 26 Districts, each governed by a **Wing** (a mega-corporation with a Singularity)
- All Wings answer to **the Head**, the supreme governing body
- Each District is split into:
  - **Nest**: Territory directly governed and protected by the Wing; safer, wealthier
  - **Backstreets**: Expansive slums outside Wing protection; dangerous, Syndicate-run
- Both halves share the same underlying culture, shaped primarily by the Wing's Singularity
- Districts grouped into four quarters (north, east, south, west) with shared cultural/climate traits
- Neighboring Districts share cultural traits - most clearly shown through Associations wearing different uniforms per quarter
- District boundaries are **unchangeable** - no variation in boundary lines or Wing letter designation
- Wings can own structures in other Districts (e.g., L Corp.'s branches across the City)
- When a Wing collapses, the District becomes ungoverned Syndicate-run territory until a new Wing takes hold
- District 26 exists but is not visible on any map - largely a mystery

### The Head, the Eye, and the Claw
The Head, the Eye, and the Claw are the three entities in direct control of A Corp., B Corp., and C Corp. respectively. The Head governs over the other Wings and retains control over the entire City, flanked by the Eye and the Claw.

**The Head:**
- Identity completely unknown - likely a group of people, not a singular entity; any detail is a closely guarded secret
- Through A Corp.: authorizes Wings, manages patents and fines, mints **Ahn** (City currency, value similar to Korean Won)
- Does not typically interfere with Wings' affairs in their own Districts
- Greatest involvement: establishing and enforcing City-wide ethics (Taboos)
- Possesses the **single deadliest military force** in the City - a couple Arbiters and Claws can destroy entire Wings and kill swarms of Outskirts monsters while coming out completely unscathed
- Controls the Sweepers directly; uses them to clean sites after Arbiter/Claw operations
- Despite thorough control over Nests, the **Five Fingers** make the Backstreets difficult to manage
- Ignores most things (inhuman experiments, Distortion exploitation) unless Taboos are broken
- Some speculate the Head preserves its "ideal image" of the City rather than maintaining order - its agents refer to their work as cultivating the "ecology" of the City

**The Eye (B Corp.):**
- Tasked with constantly surveilling the Cityfolk, watchful for threats to the Head's authority
- Gaze extends to the City's confines but typically not beyond (unless searching for a specific threat)
- Agents called **Beholders** (rarely called "Eyes")
- Not involved in physical combat - sent as static glass panels/chambers to monitor situations from a distance during Impurity operations

**The Claw (C Corp.):**
- Tasked with punishing those who break Taboos and removing threats to the City
- Agents called **Executioners** or simply **"Claws"**
- Execute offenders without any possibility of appeal
- Augmented with prosthetic heads and clawed arms with attached **Serums** using various Singularities:
  - **Serum K**: Regeneration (K Corp.'s Singularity)
  - **Serum W**: Space-bending (W Corp.'s Singularity)
  - **Serum R**: Enhanced attacks (R Corp.'s Singularity)
  - Additional Serums exist (purple-colored, possibly C Corp.'s own Singularity)
- Can teleport to/from areas when needed
- Handle lesser Taboo violations (e.g., eliminating clones past the 7-day limit)
- Accompany Arbiters during major Taboo violation operations

**Arbiters:**
- Agents of the Head directly - specialized for dealing with Impurities of the City
- Augmented through various Singularities, wield incredible power (capable of taking down entire Wings)
- Typically one Arbiter deployed at the head of a group of Claws
- Can utilize F Corp.'s fairies in combat
- Can teleport out of situations when needed
- Human appearance, uniform: long black robes with golden highlights and hexagonal patterns
- If stripped of title: all Head-related memories are wiped
- Head retrieves corpses of former/deceased Arbiters to prevent information extraction (likely applies to Claws and Beholders too)

### Key Taboos (Head-enforced, punishment = death/shutdown)
- Sapient non-human beings within the City (the "age of humanity") - driven out to the Outskirts
- AI that resembles humans (body or intelligence) - the **AI Ethics Amendment**
  - Note: if an unauthorized sapient AI finds a means of becoming human, the Head would no longer consider their existence as breaking the Taboo
- Resurrection of human beings (though time-manipulation revival seems permitted)
- Firearm creation/distribution restrictions (including limits on Workshops)
- Human cloning (two copies can exist for max 7 days per A Corp.'s time scale)
- Not paying taxes
- Violating residential areas during the Night in the Backstreets
- Surveillance/recordings during the Night in the Backstreets (including investigating incidents)
- Unauthorized usage of patented technologies
- Does NOT include: non-sapient monsters (Whales, Trash Crabs), monstrous creatures of human origin (Distortions, Abnormalities, Bloodfiends) - whether something breaks a Taboo is ultimately up to the Head
- Breaking a City Taboo =/= breaking a Nest Taboo (Nest = chased by Taboo hunters; City = death sentence; for Wings = stripped of qualifications and immediate shutdown)

### Wings & Nests

**Overview:**
- 26 Wings, each the highest authority in its District
- Directly oversee all facets of Nest life; generally leave Backstreets to fend for themselves
- Each Wing has unique cultural style, social structure, and policies that determine way of life in both Nest and Backstreets
- Differing degrees of presence, influence, and benevolence toward Backstreets - but relationship is ultimately exploitative even for cooperative Wings
- Only entities rivaling Wing power: the **Five Fingers** (who have designated all Backstreets as their territory - one reason Wings neglect them)
- Wing's position comes from its **Singularity** and ability to use it commercially; guard Singularity info with lethal force

**Feathers (Wing Citizens/Employees):**
- Joining a Wing = "optimal path in life" for Backstreets residents (financial stability, security, social refuge)
- Life under a Wing is not truly free - constant fear of insufficient results and eviction
- Wings tax feathers separately from Head taxes; inability to pay = eviction
- Highly selective entrance exams (mental prowess + other factors); entire "examinee towns" exist for studying
- Nest access requires migration permits (expensive) or visas (more common)

**Taboos:**
- Each Wing's laws are called Taboos, enforced by **Taboo hunters** (specialized Fixers)
- Capture and/or kill violators in both Backstreets and Nest
- Every District has Taboos, but enforcement and punishment vary by Wing

**Relations with Fixers/Syndicates:**
- Wings can hire Offices, Associations, and even Syndicates for tasks they don't specialize in or want distance from
- Being hired by a Wing = top honor (represents trust); Wings offer high payouts
- Wings sometimes hire Syndicates for criminal projects they can't be directly linked to

**Relations Between Wings:**
- *Competition:* Wings often need multiple Singularities to operate - purchase tech from each other (extremely expensive). Corporate espionage and theft are common. **Patent wars** = lawsuits over patents that often escalate to physical warfare; neither side benefits (true winners are uninvolved spectators who profit from revealed info)
- *Collusion:* Partnerships and exchanges of supplies/personnel are common, especially for hard-to-replicate services. Example: L Corp. (energy) + W Corp. (transportation) + R Corp. (security) formed a partnership. Wings can invest in others (L Corp. funded T Corp.'s Singularity development)

**Wing Collapse:**
- Can occur from: running afoul of the Head, Singularity incidents, commercial failure, other causes
- Nest thrown into chaos, slowly becomes indistinguishable from Backstreets
- Syndicates and other organizations rush in to claim holdings
- Eventually a new Wing rises, is assigned the Nest by the Head, buys or forcibly reclaims it
- Former feathers generally do not fare well - forced to relocate to other Nests or join the Backstreets
- Collapsed Wing's Singularity becomes publicly available (patent expires)

### Backstreets

**Overview:**
- Areas of a District NOT included in the Nest, outside Wing protection
- Dangerous, poverty-ridden slums - nigh-impossible to find a resident who enjoys it
- Not split evenly with Nests - stretch all over the City in a **vein-like distribution**
- Not always literal streets: can take forms like murky wetlands (District 21)
- Roads are dangerous and roundabout, but offer shortcuts and no vehicular traffic
- Culture varies dramatically by District (e.g., District 23's Backstreets = penchant for cannibalism)
- Function under their own set of rules and ideas, separate from Nest culture
- Still supply amenities: restaurants, clothing stores, dry cleaners, groceries
- Technology available but inconsistent - ranges from rudimentary to high-tech depending on what one can afford

**Relationship with Nests:**
- Backstreets exist as **motivation**: Backstreets citizens work to reach the Nest; Nest citizens work to avoid being kicked to the Backstreets
- Extremely difficult for Backstreets-born to make it into Nests - may resort to extreme measures for migration permits
- Nest citizens are aware of their advantage - frequently manifests as **belittlement**, not sympathy (observing Backstreets struggles as entertainment is a trend)
- Nest matters considered exponentially more important than Backstreets matters (disasters in Backstreets go months without response; same in a Nest = immediate elite response)
- Nest citizens earn roughly **6x more Ahn** than Backstreets citizens
- Divides range from chain fences to invisible plasma walls - even the weakest border deters crossing
- Wings still influence Backstreets culture through policies (some Wings cooperative, others thoroughly exploitative)

**Factions of the Backstreets:**
- Considered foolish to not belong to some group - individuals are easy targets
- **Rats**: The lowest of the low - "lowly gangsters" with no real power, surviving day-by-day, respected by no one, hardly a Syndicate
- **Fixers & Offices**: Fixers need a license from Hana (requires physical fitness, minimum age 20). Most join Offices. **Workshops** = specialized Offices that craft/sell weapons, tools, armor
- **Syndicates**: General term for organized groups not bound by Fixer regulations. Reputation for thugs and criminal activity. Not all are bad-intentioned (some solve problems, hunt other Syndicates). Can be hired by Offices, Associations, or even Wings. Can be subsidiaries of larger Syndicates
- **The Five Fingers** ("Hand of the Backstreets"): Thumb, Index, Middle, Ring, Pinky. Power rivals the Wings. Each has distinct culture and conditions for protection:
  - Thumb: values respect for superiors
  - Index: anyone can receive protection if they follow the Prescripts given by Messengers
  - Middle: values cordialness and common courtesy
  - All Backstreets are designated as Five Fingers' territory (a major reason Wings neglect them)

### The Night in the Backstreets
- **81-minute phenomenon** from **3:13 AM to 4:34 AM**, every night without fail
- During this period, anything goes - no one held responsible for crimes committed
- Recording anything is **strictly forbidden** - eyewitness testimony is the only permissible account
- Anyone who tries to report on illegal activity during this period will be targeted and silenced by the Claw
- Considered brutal, appalling, and barbaric even for the City
- Popular for settling grudges and as a tool for murder (leave corpses/weapons outside for Sweepers)
- **Sweepers** appear in **three waves**, marching from one end of the Backstreets to the other, consuming everything alive or dead within their reach
- Only way to avoid Sweepers: stay indoors until the final wave passes
- Two Taboos: residential areas cannot be violated; nothing during the Night is recorded
- Can drag Nest citizens into Backstreets to dispose of them during the Night
- Generally a poor idea for anyone to be out in the Backstreets at night

### Nests

**Overview:**
- Segregated portion of a District, controlled by a Wing
- Wealthier, cleaner, safer than Backstreets (though still prone to tragedy)
- Primary population: higher-class citizens, those allowed per District laws, and "feathers" (Wing employees)
- Ultimate purpose: protect the "eggs" (Singularities used by the conglomerates)
- Run by taxes and money each Wing makes via Singularity
- Each Nest is vastly different - distinct cultures shaped by their Wing
- Many feature strict entry guidelines, frustrating immigration services, and lockdown protocols
- Nest-affiliated Fixers can be called in to clear blocking elements during lockdowns

## Fixers & Associations

### Fixers
- Mercenaries for hire - can be contracted for anything from menial tasks to active combat
- Grading system: Grade 9 (lowest) to Grade 1 (highest)
- Most congregate in **Fixer Offices** (small to mid-sized businesses)
- Virtually impossible to do Fixer work without augmentation procedures
- **Color Fixers**: Grade 1 Fixers of exceptional renown, considered beyond the grading system. Titled by Hana Association. Considered to live lives of "true freedom."

### Associations
- **12 Associations** in the City (not 13 - Hana oversees the system but is itself one of the 12)
- Each specializes in a different field; cases are sorted by type and sent to the relevant Association
- Associations delegate requests to their **Associate Offices** (affiliated Fixer Offices) or **Associate Fixers** (direct employees)
- Working for an Association = stable salary, requests handed to you; but must accept every assigned request regardless of risk
- City divided into four quarters (N/E/W/S); each Association has four **branches** by quarter
- Branches can differ in uniforms, weapons, traditions, battle techniques, and clothing (climate-dependent)
- Within each branch: **6 Sections** (Section 1 = most prestigious/well-paid, Section 6 = least)
- Leadership: **President** (Association) > **Branch Manager** (branch) > **Director** (section)

### Syndicates
- Unregulated counterpart to Fixer Offices - comparable to gangs
- Range from small corner groups to City-wide organizations
- Can be hired for contract jobs (higher risk than Fixers, no regulations)
- Wings sometimes hire them for criminal projects
- Many replicate family dynamics
- Graded through the City's hazard system
- **Five Fingers**: The five largest Syndicates, power rivaling Wings, "the hand of the Backstreets"

## Culture & Society

### Currency & Economy
- **Ahn** - common currency (value similar to Korean Won)
- 4 million Ahn/month = upper middle class
- Great income gaps between Backstreets and Nest, and within the Nest itself
- Money believed to solve any problem

### Fashion (by quarter)
- **South**: 21st century western formal (exception: T Corp. = 19th century steampunk)
- **East**: Eastern/Chinese fashion, traditional to modern, cloth-based
- **North**: Heavy, loose-fitting clothing (colder weather), excessive decorations
- **West**: Leather over cloth, metal armor, medieval European inspired

### Languages
- 13 common languages (likely corresponding to the 12 Association languages + 1)
- Other languages exist (Sweeper language, extinct languages)

### City People - Mentality
- Constant struggle to stay afloat at every level of society
- Outskirts people dream of the City; Backstreets people strive for the Nest; Nest people struggle to keep their place
- People tie themselves to organizations for security - willing to exploit others beneath them
- Avoid forming strong personal bonds (loved one's death = dangerous liability)
- Death and mass casualties are common and normalized (80,000 casualties = not particularly shocking)
- Citizens are apathetic, uncaring, emotionally disconnected
- Carmen described this as a **"disease of the mind"**

## Technology

### Body Modification
- **Prosthetic substitution**: Replace organic parts with mechanical/bionic ones - can replace entire body except the brain
- **Augmentation**: Superhuman speed, strength, impervious body parts - virtually required for Fixer work
- Sight of mechanical limbs or display-faces is incredibly common
- Available at various quality levels (expensive/safe for Nest, cheap/side-effects for Backstreets)

### Life Insurance
- Policies (e.g., H Corp.) allow resuscitation after death in a new body
- Brain must remain intact
- Trivializes pain and death for those who can afford it

### Healthcare
- Nearly every health problem theoretically curable through Wing tech
- In practice, only affordable for the wealthy

### General Tech
- Cell phones, Internet, public transport exist but not universally available
- Varies by District (e.g., T Corp.'s District uses letters instead of advanced communication)

### Singularities
- Unique, fantastical technology that breaks the laws of physics
- What makes a corporation qualify as a Wing
- Closely guarded secrets; products are widespread
- Must be converted into marketable products for profit
- **Every Singularity fundamentally requires human suffering to function**
- If a Wing collapses, patents expire and anyone can use the tech (loses Singularity status)
- Examples: L Corp.'s Enkephalin, K Corp.'s "Tearful Thing", T Corp.'s time control

## Hazards
Graded by how much people are willing to pay to eliminate the threat (least to most):
1. **Urban Myth**
2. **Urban Legend**
3. **Urban Plague**
4. **Urban Nightmare**
5. **Star of the City**
6. **Impuritas Civitatis** (Impurity of the City) - Head-assigned only

Hana Association assigns Myth through Star levels. Head manages Impurities directly, usually banishing them to the Outskirts.

## Monsters & Phenomena

### Monsters
- Major threat throughout and outside the City
- Includes Abnormalities, Distortions, trash crabs, gnomes, Bloodfiends, etc.
- Many Outskirts monsters are man-made creations banished from the City
- More common in Outskirts than within the City

### Abnormalities
- Humans-turned-beasts: bizarre, unkillable creatures reflecting fears, beliefs, desires, and stories
- Production streamlined by Lobotomy Corporation
- Vary greatly in strength and capacity for communication/sentience
- Following L Corp.'s downfall, remaining Abnormalities inhabit abandoned branch facilities

### Distortions
- Transformed humans caused by extreme mental collapse + accepting a mysterious disembodied voice (Carmen's)
- Physically embody negative emotions (despair, self-hatred, fury)
- Unlike Abnormalities, maintain traces of who they were and can be killed
- Can be converted back to human with the right approach and fast enough action
- Will become an Abnormality if left long enough
- Began appearing after the White Nights and Dark Days

### E.G.O. (Extermination of Geometrical Organ)
- Denying Carmen's voice during mental distress allows manifesting complementary armor, weapons, or more
- Increasingly common spectacle in the City

### Sweepers
- Patented liquid in humanoid metal tanks - their true body is melted-down humans
- Must convert other humans into fuel to sustain existence
- Predictable schedule during Night in the Backstreets
- Can be exploited as tools for murder/crime cleanup

## History

### Timeline
- City has existed for minimum 400 years
- Story of Limbus Company begins January 984 (reference epoch unknown)
- 400 years ago: Districts already established, Fixers/Syndicates/Associations existed, advanced tech already present

### The Smoke War (~974)
- Only mentioned Wing War
- Started by prospective Lobotomy Corporation + Udjat leader to topple Old L Corp.
- Old L Corp. had steep energy prices and pollution
- Several Wings joined the effort
- Lobotomy Corporation triumphed, became the new L Corp.
- Cleaner, cheaper energy - considered to have "freed" citizens from the smoke

### The White Nights and Dark Days
- Lobotomy Corporation's Seed of Light Project (founded by Carmen, completed by Ayin)
- Goal: cure the "disease of the mind" by spreading Light across the City for 7 days
- Angela (illegal AI) stole the Light after 3 days - remaining 4 days plunged into darkness
- Results: stronger emotions and connections (as Carmen hoped), but also:
  - The Distortion Phenomenon
  - Greater ease of manifesting E.G.O.
  - Carmen targeting mentally collapsed individuals to turn them into beasts (Distortions)

## Known Districts

### Southern Quarter

**District 4:**
- Decrepit and dreary Backstreets with large population of Old G Corp. members and low-level thugs
- Decaying urban jungle with a gloomy forest area

**District 10 (J Corp. - "Nest of Gambling"):**
- Luck governs everyday life - "Fortune Wheels" determine decisions and values
- Powerful Backstreets Syndicates (Los Mariachis, Tingtang Gang, Tieqiu Crew, Yurodiviye)
- Glitzy casinos alongside rundown scrapyards; heavy Oufi Association presence
- Gambling-based economy; underground mines using slave labor for those who failed to pay debts
- Great strain on the concept of fairness

**District 11 (K Corp.):**
- Known for HP Ampules (healing vials from K Corp.'s Singularity)
- Advanced technology, modern/futuristic appearance
- One of the safest Nests; heavy immigration paperwork and constant security
- Backstreets full of crooks stealing visas
- Contains Calw (Sinclair's hometown, prosthetic industry holy site, destroyed by N Corp. Inquisition)
- Lobotomy Corp. branch located beneath Calw
- Exhaustive work culture with lengthy workweeks, job instability, and corporate espionage

**District 12 (L Corp. - COLLAPSED):**
- Previously owned by Lobotomy Corporation
- Following Wing collapse: disputed land fought over by major factions
- L Corp. had established branches across many Districts for energy distribution
- Branches now house Golden Boughs and assorted abandoned tech
- Current state unknown; remains unclaimed

**District 20 (T Corp. - TimeTrack Corporation) [CAMPAIGN SETTING]:**
- Entire District washed in sepia filter (colors purchasable for the wealthy); effect lessens at outer reaches
- Time as currency; citizens live in varying timeframes; 12 massive clocktowers per section to track actual time
- Victorian/steampunk aesthetic; citizens wear district-mandated timepieces
- **Innovation Culture:** Remarkable innovation in recent years. Technological research practiced by all walks of life. Citizens can apply for exams to become a **certified Inventor** (multiple Grades). T Corp. financially backs this and organizes the annual **Inventions Expo** (Grade 1+ Inventors showcase work). Prizes include participation trophies, "distinctive" and "exceptional" awards, and the coveted **Inventor of the Year** title (comes with remarkable prize money, highly sought by Nest and Backstreets dwellers alike)
- **Regulation:** All inventions must be registered through the **Technology Administration Agency**; use regulated through the **T Corp. Patents Agency**
- Highly industrialized - packed with factories spewing black smoke; child labor common in Backstreets; rampant worker exploitation even within Nest
- Time Collectors frequent Backstreets for Time Tax and unregistered inventions
- Yurodiviye ideology spreading in the Nest due to worker exploitation
- High immigration from District 19 (League of Nine Litterateurs, Blade Lineage)
- **Borders District 21 (U Corp.)** to the south - access to the Great Lake and the Outskirts

**District 21 (U Corp.):**
- Southernmost District, connected to the Great Lake and the Outskirts
- Great Lake: massive divided lake with unique biomes and laws per portion
- Parasitic creatures called **Whales** including "The Five Calamities" (five major Whales)
- Backstreets across five colossal **Portships** (mobile building collections attached via Singularity)
- Dangerous Syndicates (Twinhook Pirates hold tourists hostage)
- Popular tourist destination but most locations are tourist traps
- Exports: canned food preserved via Singularity, mermaid-flavored ice cream
- Monsters (Trash Crabs) lurk in Backstreets; Trash Crab wine is a commodity
- The Middle holds many properties via subsidiaries (clubs, event venues)

### Eastern Quarter

**District 8 (H Corp. - Hongyuan Bioengineering):**
- Nearly entire District is the "Hongyuan Complex" (massive construction containing both Nest and Backstreets)
- Buildings periodically shift and move based on residents' net worth
- Creates intricate maze of buildings and alleyways
- Culture: high importance on leading class status; Jia Family holds bulk of power
- **Family Hierarchy Examinations**: people born and raised solely to participate (die or return home a failure)
- Population relies on Boluses (Singularity product) - creates "regulars" (mutated people from overuse)
- Life Insurance tech (revive if head intact; overuse = degradation)
- Condemned marked as failures, involved in human testing, hidden from general population
- Kong family massacre treated as cautionary tale; songs of their failures sung in Backstreets
- Nest ("Daguanyuan"): massive crimson-roofed pagoda; intense interfamily sabotage

**District 9 ("Streets of Music"):**
- Location of the most well-known Distortion Phenomenon case (the Pianist)
- Metropolitan buildings with prominent skyscrapers

**District 17 (Q Corp.):**
- Creates glyphs placed on cursed talismans (strengthen weapons, create fake buildings, etc.)
- Implied to house a location relevant to The Pinky

**District 18 (R Corp.):**
- Easternmost District; base of R Corp.'s private military
- Architecture bears no resemblance to neighbors (resembles District 23 instead)
- Dark metallic walls with orange highlights; Hatcheries (woods-like areas where soldiers are cloned)
- Income from Wing partnerships (notably former partnership with L Corp.)
- Facing monetary troubles since L Corp.'s collapse
- Frequently works as commissioned mercenaries/private armies

### Northern Quarter

**District 14 (N Corp. - Nagel und Hammer):**
- White-clad District of plain buildings
- Fosters those seeking human experiences; accomplished educational departments
- Grim commodities: suicide booths, "Canned Experiences" (collected feelings consumed by others)
- Factions: New League of Nine Litterateurs, N Corp. Inquisition (genocidal extermination of prosthetics users)

**District 16 (P Corp.):**
- Buildings highly elastic and resistant (P Corp.'s Singularity); art deco aesthetic
- Backstreets home to La Manchaland (blood-made amusement park of Bloodfiends, appears/disappears regularly)

**District 25:**
- Northernmost and coldest District
- Backstreets: chilly, derelict streets with garbage and snow
- Especially low standard of living compared to Nest
- The Middle holds great power over Backstreets; most residents can't live comfortably
- Hometown of Rodion and Sonya; birthplace of the Yurodiviye Syndicate (response to wealth disparity)

### Western Quarter

**District 13 (M Corp. - MDM Entreprise):**
- Produces Moonlight Stones (protect from psychological damage)
- Rehabilitation facilities
- Liu Association known for using Wing's product
- Offers for-hire and District-sponsored Fixer collectives to assist other Districts

**District 19 (S Corp. - Salpippyeo Agroindustries):**
- Korean/Joseon-inspired architecture; natives known for verbose, flowery speech
- Agriculture and livestock focus
- Ruled by feudal aristocracy with Royal Court and King
- Corrupt officials allowing violent Syndicates (Kurokumo Clan) to take territory
- Specific roles: "Blade of Clouds" (Un'geom, best swordsmen), Ch'unokkun (tenacious hunters under Royal Court)
- High emigration rate due to turmoil; rarely shown except at night under moonlight
- Home of the League of Nine Litterateurs and Blade Lineage Syndicate

**District 22 (V Corp.):**
- Claustrophobic Nest with expansive waterways and European architecture
- Led by "Elders of V Corp."
- 80,000 Nest citizens killed in a Distortion event - sparked widespread interest in Distortions
- Wing now investing in hiring Fixers to gather Distortion intel

**District 23 (W Corp. - WARP Corp.):**
- Westernmost District; major hub for WARP technology/trains
- Backstreets infamous for **human meat food culture** - Syndicates kidnap civilians for restaurants
- Eight Chefs: faction of manic cannibal cooks respected by restauranteurs (possibly inactive)
- Washed in dark red light at times
- W Corp. currently declining; unknown Wing working with Limbus Company to create a Singularity to cause collapse

**District 24 (X Corp.):**
- Creates incredibly durable metals from alloy excavation sites
- "Hellholes": excavation sites home to monsters, closely border the Nest
- Items found fully formed as-is (can excavate a fully built microwave)
- Nest residents sometimes get trapped while scavenging

### Unknown/Special

**District 26:**
- Absent from all maps; occasionally left out of discussions
- Confirmed to have a Nest and be managed by a Wing
- Nothing else is known
