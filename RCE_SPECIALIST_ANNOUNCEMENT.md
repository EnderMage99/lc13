# R-Corp Expedition Force: Specialist Update Preview

## The Next Evolution of Combat

The R-Corp Expedition Force is evolving. No longer will our Rooks rely on standard-issue equipment alone. Through recovered technology and biological research, we're introducing a revolutionary system that transforms ordinary soldiers into **specialized weapons of war**.

---

## 🧬 The Research System: Hunt. Harvest. Evolve.

Every enemy you face is now a potential upgrade. Using the new **R-Corp Biological Harvester**, mark your targets before elimination to extract valuable biological samples. These samples fuel a branching research tree where **every body part matters**.

### The Trait Economy
Each harvested body part carries traits that define its value:
- **Common Traits:** Organic, Mechanical, Fodder - Found on basic enemies
- **Valuable Traits:** Weaponized, Armored, Agile - From specialized units
- **Rare Traits:** Elite, Corrupted, Psionic - Only from bosses and special enemies

### Smart Research Feeding
Each research project has trait preferences:
- **Favored Traits** boost part value by up to 50% - An Elite part worth 35 points becomes 52
- **Negative Traits** reduce value by up to 50% - That Fodder part might only give 5 points
- **Required Traits** are mandatory - No Mechanical trait? The project rejects the part entirely

**Example:** Heavy Flamethrower needs 150 points. Feed it Weaponized X-Corp parts for fast progress, or waste twice as many Fodder parts. Your choice.

### Strategic Depth
- Hunt specific enemies for their traits (X-Corp are Organic, Clan are Mechanical)
- Save rare Elite parts (Gained from Corrupers and other powerful foes) for Tier 3 weapons that require them
- Choose whether to rush one specialist path or develop all three
- Wrong parts in the wrong research = wasted resources

## 🔥 Hellfire Rooster: *"Become the Inferno"*

**The Fantasy:** You are a walking wildfire, immune to your own flames, turning battlefields into burning hellscapes.

**The Power:**
- Set the world ablaze with persistent fire zones that last 30 seconds
- Walk through your own inferno unharmed while enemies burn
- Demolish fortifications with triple damage against structures

**Signature Weapons:**
- **Heavy Flamethrower** - Piercing fire streams that ignite everything in a line
- **Inferno Rush Blade** - Dash through enemies (50 fuel per dash), leaving burning trails
- **Inferno Wall Projector** - Create 5-tile fire barriers (100 fuel each) for area denial
- **Auto-Defense Turret** - Attached to your suit, automatically targets hostiles

**The Cost:** Your 1000-capacity fuel tank burns through resources fast. Without Raven support or fuel stations, you're just a Rook with fancy armor. Strategic fuel management separates living legends from burnt-out casualties.

**Perfect for players who love:** Area control, destruction, and watching the world burn.

---

## 🐍 Venom Rattlesnake: *"Patient. Deadly. Inevitable."*

**The Fantasy:** You're a trap-laying predator who marks prey with escalating venom, then strikes with devastating force.

**The Power:**
- Stack up to 10 venom marks on enemies (2 DPS per stack)
- Weapons deal exponentially more damage to venomed targets (up to 250% with Miasma Field)

**Signature Weapons:**
- **Toxic Mine** - 3-second setup, proximity detonation, applies 5 venom stacks instantly
- **Venom Spike Strips** - Deploy across corridors, 4 stacks + immobilize on contact
- **Plague Scythe** - Spinning melee weapon with 2-tile reach, +5 damage per venom stack
- **Miasma Field Generator** - Ultimate area denial, +25% damage per stack in the toxic zone

**The Cost:** Your 500-capacity acid tank limits your engagement time. Every trap placed, every shot fired depletes your reserves. Without preparation time and acid resupply, you're helpless. This class rewards planning over aggression.

**Perfect for players who love:** Preparation, multiplication mechanics, and tactical positioning.

---

## ⚡ Storm Ram: *"Unstoppable Force Meets Immovable Object"*

**The Fantasy:** You are living lightning - rushing into melee combat, devastating groups, then escaping before retaliation.

**The Power:**
- +100 Fortitude makes you incredibly tanky
- Automatic speed boosts after attacks for guaranteed escape

**Signature Weapons:**
- **Thunder Gauntlets** - Basic attacks chain lightning, dash attack for 20 charge
- **Lightning Ram** - 40-charge devastating charge that passes through structures
- **Thunderclap Gauntlets** - 50-charge AoE burst with automatic retreat dash
- **Railgun Charge** - 75-charge ultimate: become invulnerable, pierce through everything for 120 damage

**The Cost:** Your 1000-capacity capacitor drains with every ability. Storm Rams live for the rush - literally. Without charge, you're a sitting duck in melee range with no guns. Ravens with portable power cells are your lifeline between engagements.

**Perfect for players who love:** High-risk melee combat, mobility, and feeling like an absolute tank.

## The Power and The Price

### Resource Management is Life or Death
Specialists are gods on the battlefield... until their tanks run dry. Every ability costs precious fuel, acid, or charge. A Hellfire Rooster with an empty tank can't even light a cigarette. A Storm Ram without power is just a very tough punching bag. This isn't a power fantasy without consequences - it's strategic resource warfare.

### Ravens: The Unsung Heroes
While specialists grab glory, **Ravens** keep them alive. Armed with portable fuel canisters (100 units), acid tanks, and power cells, they're the difference between a successful push and a spectacular failure. One Raven can sustain two specialists in combat. No Ravens supporting your Specialists? Their operation is doomed.

## The Choice is Yours

Each specialist class completely changes your playstyle. You'll lose access to ALL conventional weapons but gain powers that make you a force of nature. The implants are permanent - choose wisely. Work as a team or die alone.
