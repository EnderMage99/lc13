# Resurgence Clan: Village Survival Gamemode

## Overview
A 20+ player cooperative survival gamemode where players control humanoid machines living in a village within the ruins beneath the City. Players must work together to gather resources, expand their village, and survive in hostile territory while managing both individual and collective needs.

## Core Concept
Players take on different roles within a machine village, building a thriving settlement through complex production chains and social systems. Expeditions provide initial resources and rare materials to jumpstart village industries, but the heart of gameplay revolves around village life - crafting, building, socializing, and creating a sustainable mechanical civilization. The gamemode emphasizes deep village mechanics and roleplay, with expeditions serving as exciting but supplementary content.

## Lore & Setting

### Historical Background
The Resurgence Clan was once led by four Elders:
- **The Warlord** - Military leader (deceased, sacrificed defending the clan)
- **The Priest** - Spiritual leader (current village head)
- **The Weaver** - Cultural leader (allied with Priest)
- **The Tinkerer** - Technical leader (exiled for extremism)

### The Great Exodus
- The clan lived in the hostile Outskirts for thousands of years
- Upon hearing of the human City, the Warlord led them on a decades-long journey
- Upon arrival, the Head's forces nearly exterminated them
- The Warlord sacrificed themselves to allow survivors to escape into the ruins
- The clan now hides beneath the City, trapped between hostile forces above and dangers below

### Current Situation
- The Priest leads the remaining clan, believing they can one day become "human enough" to be accepted
- The Weaver supports this peaceful path
- The Tinkerer was exiled after attempting to create a weapon using citizens as fuel
- The clan struggles to survive in the ruins, scavenging and avoiding detection

## Game Mechanics

### Resource Systems

#### Charge (Short-term Resource)
- Passively regenerates over time
- Required for abilities and special actions
- Functions as a soft cooldown system
- Depletes when using mechanical abilities

#### Faith (Long-term Stat)
- Gradually decreases over time
- Drains faster during expeditions and when using abilities
- Low Faith reduces overall effectiveness and stats
- Restored through village activities:
  - Attending Priest sermons
  - Communal meals
  - Social interactions
  - Rest periods

### Village Production & Activities

#### Production Chains
Village sustainability relies on interconnected production systems that keep players engaged:

**Scrap Processing Chain**
- **Scrap Sorting Station** - Players manually sort expedition scrap into categories
- **Recycling Furnace** - Melts sorted scrap into usable metal ingots
- **Component Press** - Shapes ingots into specific parts
- **Assembly Line** - Combines parts into useful items
- Each step requires player interaction and skill

**Energy Generation**
- **Solar Collectors** - Must be cleaned and maintained regularly
- **Energy Converters** - Players balance power distribution across village
- **Battery Banks** - Require monitoring and rotation to prevent degradation
- **Emergency Generators** - Manual operation during power failures

**Synthetic Food Production**
- **Nutrient Farms** - Growing synthetic proteins requires constant adjustment
- **Processing Kitchen** - Players prepare meals with Faith-boosting properties
- **Community Dining Hall** - Social hub where meals restore both hunger and Faith

#### Village Infrastructure

**Living Spaces**
- **Personal Quarters** - Customizable rooms players can decorate and upgrade
- **Communal Areas** - Spaces for roleplay and social activities
- **Recreation Center** - Games and activities that restore Faith
- **Memory Archive** - Players can share and view clan history

**Workshop Complex**
- **Basic Crafting Stations** - For everyday items and tools
- **Advanced Manufacturing** - Complex multi-step processes for high-tier items
- **Experimental Lab** - Players can attempt to innovate new technologies
- **Repair Bay** - Constant stream of maintenance work

**Cultural Buildings**
- **Central Chapel** - Sermons, ceremonies, and community gatherings
- **Theater** - Players can perform shows to boost village morale
- **Library** - Research station for improving production efficiency
- **Monument Construction** - Long-term village project requiring collective effort

#### Daily Village Activities

**Production Jobs**
- Operating furnaces and presses
- Managing power distribution
- Tending nutrient farms
- Cooking and serving meals
- Crafting tools and equipment
- Repairing damaged infrastructure
- Building new structures
- Maintaining defensive walls

**Social Activities**
- Attending sermons (major Faith restoration)
- Participating in theater performances
- Teaching skills to other players
- Organizing village events
- Trading personal items
- Decorating communal spaces
- Sharing stories in the archive
- Playing recreational games

**Administrative Tasks**
- Managing resource inventories
- Planning construction projects
- Scheduling work shifts
- Resolving disputes
- Training new citizens
- Organizing village defense
- Maintaining supply chains
- Coordinating production queues

#### Resources Types
- **Raw Scrap** - Unprocessed materials from expeditions
- **Processed Metal** - Refined from scrap through village industry
- **Energy Cells** - Both found and manufactured in village
- **Components** - Basic parts crafted from processed materials
- **Ancient Tech** - Rare expedition finds for special projects
- **Synthetic Nutrients** - Grown and processed in village
- **Fabric Materials** - For clothing and decorations
- **Construction Supplies** - Produced through village chains

### Expedition System (Supplementary Content)

**Purpose**: Expeditions are high-risk, high-reward ventures that provide initial resources to jumpstart village production chains and occasionally yield rare materials that cannot be manufactured. They should comprise only 10-20% of gameplay time.

#### When Expeditions Occur
- **Early Game**: 1-2 expeditions to gather starting materials
- **Resource Crises**: When specific rare materials are depleted
- **Special Events**: Story-driven expeditions for unique rewards
- **Weekly Ventures**: Scheduled expeditions for variety (not necessity)

#### Expedition Lifecycle
1. **Planning Phase** (5 minutes)
   - Village votes on whether expedition is needed
   - Volunteers sign up (3-4 players maximum)
   - Village continues normal operations

2. **Quick Deployment** (10-15 minutes total)
   - Small team ventures out
   - Fast-paced exploration and combat
   - Focus on specific objectives
   - Meanwhile, village production continues

3. **Integration Phase**
   - Returned materials feed into village production chains
   - Expedition team returns to village roles
   - Stories shared during regular village activities

#### Expedition Rewards
- **Starter Materials**: Raw scrap to begin production chains
- **Rare Components**: Cannot be crafted, needed sparingly
- **Knowledge**: Blueprints for new village productions
- **Narrative Items**: Lore objects for the Memory Archive

### Character Progression

#### Skill Development
- **Production Skills**: Improve efficiency at specific village tasks
- **Social Skills**: Better Faith generation from interactions
- **Leadership Skills**: Unlock advanced administrative abilities
- **Crafting Mastery**: Create higher quality items with same materials
- Skills improve through regular village activities, not combat

#### Limb Replacement System
- Start with basic mechanical limbs
- Craft upgraded parts through village production:
  - **Artisan Arms** - Better crafting speed and quality
  - **Worker Legs** - Reduced fatigue from village tasks
  - **Social Core** - Improved Faith generation from interactions
  - **Industrial Frame** - Increased carrying capacity for production
- Limbs enhance village activities rather than combat
- Regular maintenance creates ongoing village jobs

#### Village Upgrade Projects
Instead of individual upgrades consuming rare resources, players work together on village-wide improvements:
- **Production Line Upgrades** - Faster processing for all players
- **Communal Faith Shrine** - Village-wide Faith regeneration boost
- **Advanced Workshop** - Unlocks new crafting recipes
- **Cultural Monument** - Permanent morale boost
- These projects require sustained collaborative effort over time

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Leadership Roles (Dual Leadership System)

#### The Priest (1 player) - Spiritual & Cultural Leader
- Conducts daily sermons (major village activity)
- Manages village Faith systems and morale
- Organizes religious ceremonies and rituals
- Provides ideological guidance and vision
- Mediates spiritual conflicts and faith crises
- Maintains the Chapel and sacred spaces
- Leads community prayers before meals
- Occasionally approves expeditions when necessary

#### The Weaver (1 player) - Social & Economic Leader
- **Thread System**: Manages invisible "threads" connecting all villagers
  - Tracks relationships and social bonds between players
  - Can strengthen threads through organized activities
  - Weak threads reduce village efficiency
  - Strong threads boost production and Faith regeneration
- **Production Harmony**: Oversees all production chains
  - Assigns workers to optimize efficiency based on relationships
  - Resolves work conflicts before they escalate
  - Balances resource distribution across departments
  - Coordinates multi-department projects
- **Cultural Preservation**: Maintains clan traditions
  - Manages the Memory Archive and historical records
  - Teaches traditional crafts and techniques
  - Organizes festivals that strengthen community threads
  - Preserves stories of the Great Exodus
- **Peace Keeping**: Promotes unity and cooperation
  - Mediates disputes between villagers
  - Prevents faction formation
  - Manages punishment and rehabilitation for rule-breakers
  - Ensures fair treatment of all citizens
- **Special Abilities**:
  - Can see all relationship values between players
  - "Weaving Ceremony" - Special event that massively boosts chosen relationships
  - "Thread Reading" - Can predict social crises before they occur
  - "Harmony Decree" - Temporary production boost when threads are strong

**Priest-Weaver Dynamic**:
- Both must agree on major village decisions
- Priest focuses on vertical relationships (individual-to-faith)
- Weaver focuses on horizontal relationships (individual-to-individual)
- Weekly council meetings to align village direction
- Can override each other only in their domains of expertise
- Together they represent the clan's dual nature: spiritual hope and practical survival

### Production Specialists

#### Master Craftsman (3-4 players)
- Operates advanced manufacturing equipment
- Trains others in crafting techniques
- Develops new production methods
- Maintains quality standards
- Manages workshop operations

#### Agricultural Overseer (2-3 players)
- Manages nutrient farms
- Develops new food recipes
- Coordinates meal services
- Optimizes Faith-boosting foods
- Maintains food supply chains

#### Power Engineer (2-3 players)
- Manages village power grid
- Maintains energy generation systems
- Balances power distribution
- Responds to power emergencies
- Develops energy efficiency improvements

#### Thread Keeper (2-3 players) - Works under the Weaver
- Monitors individual relationship threads
- Reports social issues to the Weaver
- Organizes bonding activities for weak threads
- Records relationship changes in the Archive
- Assists in Weaving Ceremonies

#### Infrastructure Specialist (3-4 players)
- Building construction and expansion
- Facility maintenance and repair
- Defensive wall upkeep
- Plumbing and ventilation systems
- Village layout optimization

### Civilian Roles (Remaining players)
- Rotate through different production jobs
- Learn skills from specialists
- Participate in social activities
- Contribute to communal projects
- Fill in wherever needed in village
- Occasionally volunteer for expeditions

## Roleplay Elements

### Social Systems
- **Reputation** - Standing within the village affects available roles
- **Relationships** - Bonds between machines affect Faith regeneration
- **Conflicts** - Ideological disputes about the clan's future
- **Traditions** - Mechanical rituals and customs

### Decision Points
- Whether to seek peace with humans or remain hidden
- Resource allocation between defense and growth
- Risk vs reward for dangerous expeditions
- Handling discovered humans or outsiders
- Dealing with Tinkerer sympathizers

### Environmental Storytelling
- Discovered logs and data from the Great Exodus
- Remnants of previous failed settlements
- Messages from the exiled Tinkerer
- Evidence of the City's surveillance

## Victory Conditions & Endgame

### Survival Victory
- Village survives for set duration (2-3 hours)
- Minimum population maintained
- Core structures intact
- Adequate resource stockpiles

### Prosperity Victory
- Expand village to maximum size
- All civilians upgraded
- Establish trade with other groups
- Discover safe passage deeper into ruins

### Faith Victory
- Maintain high collective Faith throughout
- Complete special sermon questline
- Unite the clan under single ideology
- Potential peaceful contact with surface

### Failure Conditions
- Population drops below critical threshold
- Village core destroyed
- Complete Faith collapse
- Discovery by hostile City forces

## Challenges & Events

### Village Crises (Primary Challenges)
These events occur within or near the village, keeping players engaged without leaving:

**Production Breakdowns**
- Critical machinery failures requiring immediate repair
- Power grid overloads during peak production
- Contamination in nutrient farms
- Supply chain disruptions
- Players must work together to fix issues before resources deplete

**Social Crises**
- Faith collapse in village sections
- Work strikes over resource allocation
- Ideological debates requiring priest mediation
- Festival planning conflicts
- Relationship breakdowns affecting production

**Infrastructure Emergencies**
- Structural damage from age or weather
- Ventilation system failures
- Water recycling breakdowns
- Defensive wall breaches (animals, not combat)
- Fire in production facilities

### External Pressures (Occasional Threats)
**Passive Dangers**
- Resource nodes depleting naturally
- Weather affecting solar collectors
- Seismic activity damaging structures
- Disease affecting nutrient crops
- These require village adaptation, not combat

**Rare Encounters**
- Trader machines offering deals
- Lost civilians seeking sanctuary
- Tinkerer's automated messages
- Distant explosions requiring investigation
- These create roleplay opportunities

### Event Frequency
- Minor village events every 10-15 minutes
- Major crisis every 30-45 minutes  
- External encounters 1-2 per session
- Expeditions only when voted necessary

## Design Philosophy

This gamemode emphasizes:
1. **Village Life over Adventure** - 80% of gameplay happens within village walls
2. **Production over Scavenging** - Sustainable systems rather than constant expeditions
3. **Social Dynamics** - Relationships and roleplay drive engagement
4. **Collaborative Building** - Shared projects create community investment
5. **Meaningful Work** - Every village job contributes to survival
6. **Emergent Storytelling** - Daily village life creates narrative
7. **Expedition as Spice** - Adventures add excitement without dominating gameplay

## Session Flow Example

### Early Game (0-30 minutes)
- Role selection and village orientation
- Quick starter expedition (5-10 minutes, 3 players)
- Establish production chains with returned materials
- Begin farming and energy generation
- First communal meal and sermon
- Set up work schedules

### Mid Game (30-90 minutes)
- Production chains fully operational
- Players settling into specialized roles
- Construction of new village buildings
- Theater performance or festival event
- Optional targeted expedition for rare materials (if needed)
- Social relationships developing
- First village upgrade project started

### Late Game (90-120+ minutes)
- Complex production recipes unlocked
- Major construction projects underway
- Village monument nearing completion
- Cultural traditions established
- Potential crisis events (power failure, food shortage)

## Village Engagement Mechanics

### Why Players Stay in the Village

**Constant Activity Streams**
- Production chains require active management (not automation)
- Each crafting step needs player input and timing
- Multiple parallel tasks prevent downtime
- Failure cascades create urgency

**Social Bonds (The Thread System)**
- Weaver tracks all interpersonal "threads" between players
- Strong threads provide mechanical benefits:
  - Faster production when working together
  - Bonus Faith regeneration during joint activities
  - Reduced fatigue when near thread partners
  - Shared skill progression bonuses
- Weak threads create problems:
  - Production penalties when forced to work together
  - Faith drain during conflicts
  - Can cascade into village-wide crises
- Threads strengthen through:
  - Working on the same production chain
  - Sharing meals together
  - Participating in Weaver's ceremonies
  - Collaborative building projects

**Personal Investment**
- Customizable living quarters
- Skill progression through village work
- Reputation system affecting role availability
- Personal crafting projects alongside communal ones

**Dynamic Environment**
- Village layout evolves based on player decisions
- New areas unlock as population grows
- Seasonal events and festivals
- Living economy with supply and demand

### Production Depth Examples

**Making a Single Advanced Tool**
1. Mine raw scrap (or get from expedition)
2. Sort scrap at sorting station
3. Operate furnace to create ingots (temperature management minigame)
4. Press ingots into basic components
5. Craft handle at woodworking station (using synthetic materials)
6. Combine at assembly station with precise timing
7. Quality check and potential rework
8. Each step can be optimized through skill and teamwork

**Preparing a Faith Meal**
1. Harvest nutrients from farms
2. Process in kitchen (recipe combinations)
3. Season with rare expedition spices
4. Cook with proper timing and temperature
5. Present aesthetically in dining hall
6. Serve during optimal social hours
7. Lead communal prayer for maximum Faith gain

## Conclusion

The Resurgence Clan gamemode transforms survival gameplay by centering it around village life rather than exploration. With expeditions serving merely as a resource injection system, players spend 80% of their time engaged in complex production chains, social interactions, and collaborative building projects within the village walls. This creates a living, breathing community where every player's contribution matters, relationships develop naturally, and the story emerges from daily life rather than external adventures. The depth of village systems ensures that players always have meaningful work to do, making the village not just a base to return to, but the heart of the entire experience.
