# The Weaver - Detailed Role Design

## Role Overview
The Weaver serves as co-leader alongside the Priest, managing the social and economic fabric of the village. While the Priest handles spiritual matters and the village's relationship with faith, the Weaver maintains the interpersonal bonds that keep the community functioning as a cohesive unit.

## Core Concept: The Thread System

### What Are Threads?
- Invisible connections between every pair of villagers
- Represent trust, friendship, and working relationships
- Dynamically change based on player interactions
- Visible only to the Weaver (and partially to Thread Keepers)

### Thread Mechanics

#### Thread Strength Levels
1. **Severed** (0-10): Active hostility, cannot work together
2. **Frayed** (11-30): Tension, -25% efficiency when paired
3. **Weak** (31-50): Neutral, no bonuses or penalties  
4. **Strong** (51-70): Good relationship, +15% efficiency
5. **Bonded** (71-90): Close friendship, +30% efficiency, shared XP
6. **Woven** (91-100): Perfect harmony, +50% efficiency, special abilities unlock

#### Thread Interactions
- Threads change based on proximity and activities
- Working together on production: +2-5 points per hour
- Conflicts or failures: -5-10 points instantly
- Shared meals: +1-2 points
- Weaving ceremonies: +10-20 points to selected threads

## Weaver Abilities

### Passive Abilities
- **Thread Sight**: See all relationship values as colored lines between players
- **Harmony Sense**: Get alerts when threads are weakening dangerously
- **Production Insight**: View efficiency predictions based on worker assignments
- **Social Prophecy**: 5-minute warning before social crises

### Active Abilities (Charge-Based)

#### Weaving Ceremony (High Charge Cost)
- Grand 10-minute ritual involving 4-8 players
- Participants gain +15-20 thread strength with each other
- Provides village-wide Faith boost
- Can only be performed once per hour
- Requires ceremonial materials from production

#### Mend Thread (Medium Charge Cost)
- Instantly repair a damaged thread between two players
- Brings any thread to minimum 40 strength
- 15-minute cooldown per pair
- Both players must consent

#### Harmony Decree (Medium Charge Cost)
- 30-minute village-wide buff when average thread strength >60
- +20% to all production
- +10% Faith regeneration
- Reduced fatigue accumulation
- Can only be used once per session

#### Thread Reading (Low Charge Cost)
- Deep analysis of one player's social network
- Reveals hidden conflicts or attractions
- Suggests optimal work partnerships
- Provides personalized relationship advice

#### Sever Thread (Emergency Only)
- Completely cut a toxic relationship
- Prevents two players from interacting for 30 minutes
- Should only be used to prevent village collapse
- Requires Priest approval

## Weaver Responsibilities

### Economic Management
- **Work Assignment Optimization**
  - Pair workers with strong threads for production bonuses
  - Separate conflicting players to prevent efficiency loss
  - Rotate assignments to build new relationships
  - Balance skill needs with social compatibility

- **Resource Distribution**
  - Ensure fair allocation based on contribution and need
  - Prevent hoarding through social pressure
  - Reward strong community contributors
  - Manage trade negotiations with any outsiders

### Social Architecture
- **Relationship Cultivation**
  - Identify isolated players and integrate them
  - Strengthen weak links in the social network
  - Prevent clique formation that excludes others
  - Build redundant social connections for stability

- **Conflict Resolution**
  - Mediate disputes before they affect production
  - Use thread data to understand conflict roots
  - Assign rehabilitation tasks for rule-breakers
  - Prevent faction formation through strategic interventions

### Cultural Preservation
- **Memory Archive Management**
  - Record significant village events
  - Preserve stories of strong threads and their formation
  - Document production innovations and techniques
  - Maintain the narrative of the clan's journey

- **Festival Organization**
  - Plan events that strengthen multiple threads simultaneously
  - Create traditions that reinforce community bonds
  - Celebrate production milestones and achievements
  - Use festivals to heal village-wide thread damage

## Weaver-Priest Collaboration

### Shared Responsibilities
- **Major Decisions**: Both must agree on:
  - Expedition approval
  - New construction projects
  - Punishment for serious crimes
  - Contact with outsiders
  - Major resource allocations

### Division of Authority
- **Priest Domain**: Faith, ideology, ceremonies, expeditions
- **Weaver Domain**: Production, relationships, economics, justice
- **Overlap Areas**: Festivals, morale, village expansion

### Conflict Resolution Between Leaders
- If leaders disagree, village votes with 60% required to override
- Each leader can veto once per session in their domain
- Weekly council meetings to align strategies
- Elder council can mediate if deadlock occurs

## Thread System Impact on Gameplay

### Production Effects
- **Strong Thread Pairs**: 
  - Faster crafting animations
  - Higher quality output chance
  - Shared tool usage without penalty
  - Can cover for each other's mistakes

- **Weak Thread Pairs**:
  - Slower production speeds
  - Higher failure rates
  - Cannot share workstations
  - Mistakes cascade to both players

### Village Dynamics
- **High Average Thread Strength** (>60):
  - Village enters "Harmony State"
  - All production receives passive bonus
  - Random positive events more likely
  - New recipes and blueprints unlock

- **Low Average Thread Strength** (<40):
  - Village enters "Discord State"  
  - Production penalties across the board
  - Higher chance of accidents and breakdowns
  - Faith drains faster for everyone

### Crisis Events
- **Thread Crisis**: When 3+ threads drop below 20 simultaneously
- **Faction Formation**: When two groups have strong internal threads but weak external ones
- **Isolation Crisis**: When one player has all threads below 30
- **Harmony Cascade**: When fixing one thread damages others

## Weaver Progression

### Skill Development
1. **Novice Weaver**: Can see threads, basic mending
2. **Apprentice**: Unlock Harmony Decree and ceremonies
3. **Journeyman**: Predictive abilities and efficiency bonuses
4. **Master**: Can maintain more simultaneous strong threads
5. **Grand Weaver**: Village-wide thread manipulation abilities

### Unique Upgrades
- **Golden Threads**: Mark special relationships for permanent bonuses
- **Thread Memory**: Threads decay slower over time
- **Empathic Resonance**: Feel thread changes without checking
- **Master's Eye**: See thread potential, not just current state

## Playing as the Weaver

### Early Game (0-30 min)
- Map initial relationships through observation
- Identify natural working pairs
- Establish baseline thread measurements
- Plan first Weaving Ceremony participants

### Mid Game (30-90 min)
- Optimize production through strategic pairing
- Resolve emerging conflicts quickly
- Strengthen critical production partnerships
- Build redundancy in social network

### Late Game (90+ min)
- Maintain harmony during crisis events
- Lead village through major decisions with Priest
- Achieve village-wide strong thread network
- Document the session's social evolution

## Interface & Tools

### Weaver's Loom (Special UI)
- Visual thread map showing all relationships
- Color coding for thread strength
- Production efficiency calculator
- Conflict prediction warnings
- Ceremony planning tool

### Thread Keeper Reports
- Daily summaries from Thread Keeper assistants
- Automatic alerts for dangerous thread changes
- Production pair recommendations
- Social event effectiveness metrics

## Conclusion

The Weaver role transforms the social aspect of the game from emergent roleplay into a mechanical system that directly impacts village success. By managing the invisible threads that connect all villagers, the Weaver ensures that the community functions as more than just a collection of individuals, but as a truly interconnected society where every relationship matters and every interaction has consequences.
